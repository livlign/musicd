The hi-hats · ideas

Hi-hats keep time: fast, crisp, high, usually on every eighth or sixteenth note, with an occasional open hi-hat that rings longer. The visual should feel crisp and quick, keep time with them, live at the edges where musicd puts high sounds, and belong to the sky. Each panel is a small Cosmos (stars, incoming dust, the bass core) driven by the same hi-hat pattern. The last panel is today's version.
