The guitar · ideas

Guitars are strummed chords (a quick sweep across six strings, down or up) and single-note lines. The visual should feel plucked and strummed, ring for as long as the chord does, read clearly next to the dust, and look different from the piano's stars. Each panel is a small Cosmos driven by the same playing: a strummed rhythm, with a short lead line every other bar. The last panel is today's version.
