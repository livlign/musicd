Play piece with light in the cracks, in the cool colours. A dark record of shards with a play wedge cut out of it. Music clicks the wedge into place, sets the record spinning and lights up the cracks from inside, with each beat travelling from the centre to the rim. In silence the light goes out, the record parks upright and the wedge waits outside, breathing softly.

What changed from B

  • Cool palette only: cyan at the centre fading to indigo at the rim, on a blue-black record instead of a purple-black one.
  • Hairline cracks. The light is a thin line inside a dark gap with a soft bloom, rather than a thick coloured stroke.
  • The light moves: each beat sends a bright pulse from the spindle out along every crack, and the rim flares when it arrives.
  • Real record detail: fine grooves, a label ring, and a fixed reflection that stays still while the record turns under it.
  • Silence has a pose: parked upright, cracks dark, wedge out and breathing softly, like a standby light.
  • Favicon gets its own simplified drawing, so it still reads at 16 px.
